About Us

Built for smarter grocery shopping

FreshMart is a demo online grocery platform created to showcase a modern shopping experience for Sri Lankan customers. It demonstrates how a local business can offer convenient delivery, premium daily essentials, and customer-friendly service.

This project also highlights an AI-powered customer support experience, helping shoppers quickly get answers about products, orders, and delivery queries using a chatbot integrated into the website.
